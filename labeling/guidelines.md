# Email Labeling Guidelines

This is the first draft for **Phase 2**.

## Step 2.1 Label Schema

We will use these labels:
- Trading
- Legal
- Gov & Regulatory Affairs
- Piplines
- Operations, Risk & Research
- Executive & Corporate
- Unsure
- Generic/Other

Use Unsure when it is hard to decide which team is responsible for the email. 

Use Generic/Other when the email is not related to a specific team, such as a personal message or lunch invitation 

---

## Step 2.2 Labeling Guidelines 

The goal is to choose the team that would most likely own or be responsible for the email.
Use the email subject and body to decide the label. 
The mailbox owner's team can help, but it should not always decide the final label.

### Trading

Use this label for emails about gas or power trading, prices, deals, positions, or market information
Example: Gas prices, Power deals, Trading positions

### Legal

Use this label for emails about contracts, agreements, legal revies, or other legal issues.
Examples: Contract revies, Agreement changes, Legal approval

### Gov & Regulatory Affairs

Use this label for emails about government policy, regulations, FERC, CPUC, or lobbying. 
Example: Regulation updates, Government policy, FERC issues

### Pipelines (ETS)

Use this label for emails about pipelines, transportation, capacity, tariffs, or pipeeline operations. 
Examples: Pipeline capacity, Transportation issues, Pipelin outage

### Operations, Risk & Research

Use this label for emails about scheduling, settlements, deal corrections, risk, research, or models.
Examples: Settlement issue, Deal correction, Risk report

### Executive & Corporate

Use this label for emails about company strategy, management, board issues, company announcements, or major company decisions. 
Examples: Company strategy, Board meeting, Company-wide announcement

---

## How to choose the Label

1. Check the mailbox owner's team.
2. Read the email subject and body.
3. Decide which team is mainly responsible for the email.
4. If the content clearly belongs to another team, use the team based on the content.
5. if it is hard to decide, use Unsure
6. If the email is not related to a specific team, use Generic/Other

## Example

**Mailbox owner:** Trading
**Email topic:** Contract review
**Label:** Legal
The email belongs to Legal because the main topic is contract review.
